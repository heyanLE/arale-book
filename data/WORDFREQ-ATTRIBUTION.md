# Japanese word frequency reference

`ja-wordfreq-3.1.1.json.gz` is a deterministic derived Zipf lookup from
[`wordfreq` 3.1.1](https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq), using its `large` Japanese
wordlist and `round(log10(frequency) + 9, 2)` for each listed form. Generated by
`scripts/build-ja-wordfreq.py`. It is reference data, not an official JLPT list
or a confidence score for manga OCR.

and lists the underlying sources and their attribution terms. This derived data
is distributed with that attribution and CC BY-SA 4.0 terms. The wordlist is a
snapshot of language use through about 2021; missing forms are **unknown**,
not proof that a term is rare or an OCR error.
