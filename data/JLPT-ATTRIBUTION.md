# JLPT vocabulary data attribution

`jlpt-vocabulary.json` was derived from `original_data/n1.csv` through `n5.csv` in
[stephenmk/yomitan-jlpt-vocab](https://github.com/stephenmk/yomitan-jlpt-vocab),
revision `b062d4e38c4bdd0950ae1d4ec55f04b176182e03`.
Only spellings, readings, and level numbers were retained; English definitions were omitted.

Stephen Kraus's project credits [Jonathan Waller's JLPT Resources](https://www.tanos.co.uk/jlpt/)
for the vocabulary data under CC BY. The upstream repository publishes derivative work under
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/); its license is copied as
`jlpt-source-LICENSE.txt`. The levels are community estimates, not official JLPT vocabulary lists.
